# Alberto Alatorre

**Co-Founder, FYS Labs** — Los Angeles, CA

Half engineer, half translator. I build AI systems, orchestrate the models behind them, and teach the people who use them.

## What I do

I co-founded **FYS Labs**, an AI technology lab, where we build two things:

- **[FixYourHR](https://fixinghr.com)** — an HR + AI agency, and **FYHR Pro**, our AI HR workspace: guided workflows, compliance tooling, and document generation for businesses that can't afford to get HR wrong. FYHR Pro is free to start at [fyhr.fixinghr.com](https://fyhr.fixinghr.com).
- **[FixYourShh](https://fixyourshh.com)** — AI-ready systems and enablement for teams: literacy, workflows, and tailored tools that make AI at work actually work.

The work runs in three moves: **Understand. Enable. Build.**

## What I build with

TypeScript · React · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Supabase · AI agents & LLM workflows

Most of my production code lives in private repositories — client systems, internal platforms, and the products above.

## Background

I've been building for the web since the Flash era — animation, graphic design, hand-rolled sites — and taking things apart even longer: game modding, homebrew, and the kind of rabbit holes that teach you how systems actually work. That maker streak never left. It just grew up into full-stack development and AI systems.

On paper: consulting experience inside some of the biggest tech organizations in the world — Meta, Intuit, Twitter, and PIMCO (experience, not endorsements) — alongside years in learning & development, organizational development, and program management. In the classroom: Communication Studies professor and former award-winning speech & debate coach. MA in Communication Studies (CSU Long Beach), MBA (University of the People).

The through-line is translation: deep tech, made usable by actual humans. Bilingual in English and Spanish. Based in Los Angeles & Orange County.
